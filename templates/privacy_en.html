Privacy Policy

How we handle personal data — especially children’s photos — with care.

Last updated: {{ privacy_updated }}

At Lony we respect our customers’ privacy and handle personal data, especially children’s photos and information, carefully and responsibly. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and related rights.

1. Data we collect

When ordering a Lony product, we may ask for:

  • Parent/guardian name
  • Phone number and WhatsApp
  • Child’s name and age
  • Child’s photo
  • Preferred theme or character
  • Governorate, address, and delivery details
  • Notes needed to fulfill the order

We only collect what is needed to fulfill the order, communicate with you, and provide related services.

2. Use of the child’s photo

The child’s photo is used to design and produce a personalized coloring book or other customized Lony product you ordered.

Sending a photo to Lony does not mean automatic consent for ads or social media use.

3. Accuracy of information

You are responsible for the accuracy of the data and photo you provide to Lony.

4. Marketing use

If Lony wants to use the child’s photo or final design in:

  • Ads or marketing
  • Social media
  • Public samples or portfolios

we will ask for clear consent first.

5. AI and design tools

Lony may use AI and digital design tools to help transform the child’s photo into coloring pages and personalized designs for the ordered product.

6. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or rent children’s photos or customers’ personal data for commercial purposes. Data may be shared only when needed with service providers (for example printing or delivery), under appropriate safeguards.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

8. WhatsApp communication

We may use WhatsApp to:

  • Confirm order details
  • Share previews for approval
  • Coordinate delivery

9. Third-party services

Some tools we use (payments, messaging, hosting) have their own privacy policies. Please review those policies when relevant.
